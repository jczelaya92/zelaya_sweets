Our Story

Baked by Zelaya, made for you

Zelaya Sweets started in a home kitchen by faith — a small step taken to bring glory to God. I love Jesus, and I wanted my baking to be one way of bringing a little sweetness into other people's lives, and honoring Him in the process.

That's why every cookie is made by hand, in small batches, with real ingredients — no shortcuts, no mixes. From the classic comfort of chocolate chip to flavors like ube, matcha, and tiramisu, each one is made with care, and a prayer that it blesses whoever it reaches.
